Detailed Description

Wraps the "buffer" or "abuffersrc" filter: frames or samples written here enter the graph. The source is typed by the filter it wraps, and the typed write functions refuse data of the other kind.

Examples
api2-decode-filter-encode.cpp, and api2-decode-overlay-encode.cpp.
