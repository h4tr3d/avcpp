Detailed Description

template<typename Clazz, Direction _direction>
class av::VideoCodecContext< Clazz, _direction >

Width, height, pixel format, GOP size and bit rate are plain accessors over AVCodecContext fields: they do no I/O, and they are ignored once the codec is open where FFmpeg does not allow changes.
