Detailed Description

A packet belongs to exactly one stream: it carries the stream index, the presentation and decoding timestamps, the duration and the flags of one compressed unit. Reading gives packets, writing consumes them; decoding turns them into frames and encoding turns frames back into packets.

Buffers are allocated with the FFmpeg allocator and always carry the zeroed AV_INPUT_BUFFER_PADDING_SIZE bytes after the payload. This is not optional: some muxers and parsers read payloads as C strings, and the ASS muxer does exactly that.
