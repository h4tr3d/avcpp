Detailed Description

Everything that holds uncompressed data. av::VideoFrame and av::AudioSamples share the av::Frame interface; av::PixelFormat, av::SampleFormat and av::ChannelLayout describe the data inside them; av::VideoRescaler and av::AudioResampler convert between representations.
