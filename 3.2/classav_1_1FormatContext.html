Detailed Description

The context owns the streams and the streams own the packets that come out of them. Reading is a loop of readPacket()/writePacket(); the container itself is opened once and closed by the destructor.

The context is not copyable: it owns resources and FFmpeg refers to it by pointer, so moving it would invalidate the relationships between the streams, the packets and the IO layer.

Examples
api2-decode-audio.cpp, api2-decode-encode-audio.cpp, api2-decode-encode-video.cpp, api2-decode-filter-encode.cpp, api2-decode-overlay-encode.cpp, api2-decode-rasample-audio.cpp, api2-decode.cpp, api2-demux-seek.cpp, api2-generate-subtitles.cpp, api2-hw-decode.cpp, api2-hw-encode.cpp, api2-hw-scale.cpp, api2-hw-transcode.cpp, api2-remux.cpp, api2-scale-video.cpp, api2-send-receive.cpp, and api2-subtitles.cpp.
