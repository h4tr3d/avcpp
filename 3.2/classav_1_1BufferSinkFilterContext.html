Detailed Description

Wraps the "buffersink" or "abuffersink" filter: frames or samples leave the graph here. A sink can produce zero, one or several outputs after a single push, so reading in a loop until it returns false is the normal pattern.

Examples
api2-decode-filter-encode.cpp, and api2-decode-overlay-encode.cpp.
