Detailed Description

The samples follow the layout of their format: planar formats keep one plane per channel, packed formats interleave the channels in the first plane. isPlanar() reports which one this buffer uses.

Examples
api2-decode-audio.cpp, api2-decode-encode-audio.cpp, and api2-decode-rasample-audio.cpp.
