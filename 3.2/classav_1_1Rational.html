Detailed Description

Widths, heights and frame rates in FFmpeg are rationals, and so are the time bases that give meaning to timestamps. The class keeps the FFmpeg representation and adds comparison and arithmetic that account for different denominators, so 1/25 and 2/50 compare equal.

A denominator of zero is preserved rather than corrected: it is what FFmpeg uses for "unknown", and isValid() reports it.
